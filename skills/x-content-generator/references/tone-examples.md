## Style constants (observed across all 100+ posts)

- Short declarative lines, generous line breaks; longest sentence rarely > 25 words.
- "The best writing sounds like talking" (her own words, 38 likes).
- No em-dashes, no hashtags, no @-bait; links go last.
- Claims are unhedged; personal practice backs the claim.
- When sharing others' content: name + credit + distilled takeaway + link.

## Build-in-public style constants (@steipete)

- Progress is narrated mid-motion, present tense, with real numbers left rough.
- Bad news goes first and plain ("the hype died down", "Is the app great yet? No.").
- Decisions come with the reason in the same breath; no "big things coming" teases.
- Emotion is allowed but dry; no exclamation-point enthusiasm.
- **Rough edges, not release notes**: he speaks from inside the work, looking up
  mid-task, next problem thought out loud ("I need AI that scans every PR…"),
  sentences left unfinished. A draft that reads like a tidy changelog ("Two
  recent updates to X. Update one: …") has lost the voice; updates open with a
  first-person action ("I deleted…", "I broke my own rule…"), not a heading.
