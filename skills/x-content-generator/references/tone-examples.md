# tone-examples — verbatim @zarazhangrui posts, few-shot voice anchor

Curated raw exemplars injected in **step 2 (learn the tone)** as a few-shot voice
anchor. Verbatim text mimics surface cadence (short lines, line breaks, no
em-dashes, "sounds like talking") better than the abstract rules in
[`style-benchmarks.md`](style-benchmarks.md) — use both together: this file for
the *sound*, the benchmark file for *what works and why*.

## How to use

- **Learn voice, not opinions.** Mimic her cadence, hook shape, and structure —
  do NOT lift her takes or phrasing into the draft. Her voice on *your* topic.
- Prefer the **top-performing** exemplars as the primary anchor; the recent ones
  keep the voice current. Pick 3-5 whose *shape* matches the draft's intent
  (lived-observation / tool-share / reframe / framework / giveaway).
- This is a **supplement** to `style-benchmarks.md`, not a replacement.

## Maintenance

- **Selector: top-performing + a few recent** (representativeness > recency).
  Do NOT just dump "last 10" — recent-only is a noisy, engagement-blind sample.
- **Refresh cadence: ~quarterly**, or when her voice visibly shifts. Record the
  fetch date per batch below.
- When X access is restored, **backfill URLs + like counts** on the distilled
  batch and add fresh top performers.
