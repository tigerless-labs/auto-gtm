# X topic style: distill valuable substance from the session — share+@author, or original reflection/insight

The target platform is **X/Twitter** (not Xiaohongshu). The core is "**valuable substance**," and the content splits into two types:
- **Share-type + attribution @**: a tool/product/person surfacing in the session → share it and @ its author (this very session is an example — comparing Codex/Claude browser use, unearthing content-collector-skill, agent-reach).
- **Reflection-type / original insight**: reflections, methodologies, and valuable viewpoints distilled from this exploration (not necessarily attached to a specific tool/person, not forced to @; but **it's better if there are related posts/bloggers to @**).

Accordingly, the skill needs to do three things:
- **Entity extraction**: tools/products/people mentioned in the session → map to their X handle (mandatory for share-type; also @ for reflection-type when there are related posts/bloggers)
- **Insight extraction**: what is the "substance" of this conversation — one conclusion that stands on its own, or a reflection worth stating
- **Value gate**: not every session has a publishable topic; if there's no real substance, skip it, don't force it

(This is the human's product judgment, with no external evidence backing it for now; it is benchmarked against the "distill topics from conversation" step that Lingzao lacks, see the [Lingzao manual](../sources/incumbent/lingzao-manual.md).)
